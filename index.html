About Me

Hi! I'm Hash, a Minecraft Bedrock addon developer, texture artist. I specialize in creating custom experiences for Minecraft Bedrock Edition through scripting, gameplay design, pixel art textures and custom assets. My work is heavily inspired by anime, RPG mechanics and action-focused gameplay. Over the years I've developed complex systems including abilities, transformations, combat mechanics, progression systems, custom entities and configurable gameplay features using the Bedrock Script API. Beyond programming, I also create textures, icons and visual assets that help bring my projects to life, combining functionality and design into a complete experience. My goal is to push the limits of Minecraft Bedrock and build unique, polished and memorable creations for players around the world.
