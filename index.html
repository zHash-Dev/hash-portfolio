About Me

Hello! I'm Hash, an addon developer for Minecraft Bedrock and a texture artist.

I specialize in creating customized experiences for Minecraft Bedrock Edition through scripting, game design, pixel art, and personalized assets. My work is strongly inspired by anime, RPG mechanics, and action-focused gameplay.

Besides programming, I also create textures, icons, and visual assets that bring my projects to life, combining functionality and design into a complete experience. My goal is to push the boundaries of Minecraft Bedrock and create unique, polished, and memorable creations for players around the world.
